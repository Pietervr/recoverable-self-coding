T1 Validation Tracker

Entropy SI lane · updated 4 Oct 2026, 00:50 UTC · Unimog 1f3ab0cf, RSC 8cb9067 · owner record: rsc_t1_simulation_design.md §11c and §12

Problem

The T1 assay must say, from a model's activations, whether a concept is accessed in a graded or a discrete way. Its confidence interval (the cluster bootstrap) under-covers at the hardest nulls: 14 of 20 at M2S ω = 1, 3 of 20 at ω = 2, against a required 0.90.

The refitting bootstrap may repair it (17 of 20 at ω = 1 on a B = 50 probe). That repair, under the amended start policy at B = 200, is unvalidated and its cost unmeasured. Nothing here enters the SI paper; every result sets its emphasis.

Approach

  • Free compute on CHPC Lengau, programme CSCI1913: 100,000 core-hours to 23 Mar 2027. The Mac for the photograph arm. AWS untouched.
  • No row, no run. Each stage has a ledger row, Codex's review of its launch specification, and the owner's go.
  • Push hard, evaluate live: stages prepared in parallel; every job streams its log; an alarm stops the campaign at the next safe point.
  • Each result gets a figure, an interpretation, and one line on what it means for the SI paper's framing.

What each result would mean for the SI paper

  • Stage 0 cost. Whether the refit validation is feasible at all inside 144-hour jobs; if not, the SI presents the interval repair as proposed, not validated.
  • Slice. Recovery proven and the chain measured: the screen can be costed; a failure here keeps the validation off the SI's claims.
  • Screen at ω = 1. Not stopped for futility: the SI can lean on refitting as the repair, evidence pending. Stopped: the SI leads with the cluster interval's known limitation.
  • Stage A / reader. A real-model instrument that passes lets the SI frame the assay as applied, not only simulated. Stage A has a result: it passed, and Codex accepted its report as a development check on 20 images (with the macOS caveat; one two-forward bridge check comes before Stage B, which has no go). The reader has one measured run that was not admitted (job (a), 49 of 63) and one stopped, unscored run (job (b)); its qualifying run (job (c)) waits on the first revision Codex finds favourable (revision 17 is being built).
