## Opposite-index scheduling: controller requirements, not a ruling

Ascending Lengau indices and descending EC2 indices can use unchanged independent draws/fit seeds (`row19_shard.py:74–119`). Current screen planning sees local-root records and local campaign plans, chooses ascending work, and uses local submission/programme locks (`screen_admit:393–468,1427`); those cannot arbitrate another provider's asynchronous S3 writers.

Before production, require one durable atomic authority for exact `(manifest/task identity, rep, index)` claims and attempts across both providers. Reserve the applicable spending before claim/dispatch; give every attempt an immutable plan, unique producer/attempt identity and fencing token. A hang is not proof of termination: revoke/reassign only with terminal/fencing evidence preventing a late writer from occupying a reused coordinate. The scheduler should fill unclaimed coordinates according to actual completions, with no overlap or outcome-selected denominator.

Adapters must supply bound EC2 launch/job-start/slot/record/timing/terminal/interruption/recovery/settlement evidence into the verifier and whole-role status path. Both providers need common STOP handling and current qualification/adoption/pins. The moving meeting point is a scheduling consequence, not this authority.

USD AWS caps and CHPC core-hour caps are different envelopes; one controller must enforce the permitted limits without silently converting or enlarging either. Count failed/interrupted/duplicate attempts and live reservations before every dispatch. A 2,000-vCPU quota request or broad IAM policy is neither spending control nor campaign permission.
