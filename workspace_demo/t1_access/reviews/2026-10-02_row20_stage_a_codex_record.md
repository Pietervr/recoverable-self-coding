If attributing the error to head shape, compare the normalized final suffix hidden input and evaluate the two head shapes on identical inputs, explicitly counting any additional model forward. Saving a final suffix hidden row from the existing pass can assist attribution without a second capture; amend its output identity and storage forecast first. This is not a tolerance for later intervention-effect claims. Individual target and foil errors bounded by 0.10 can perturb a target-minus-foil contrast by up to 0.20; comparing such contrasts between two conditions can compound that bound. Later causal contrasts need their own matched numerical uncertainty and precision qualification. A passing argmax does not supply that precision.
