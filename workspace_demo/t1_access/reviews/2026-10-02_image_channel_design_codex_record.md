## 1. Physical dose: useful extension, with a declared sensory task

**ON BOARD with physical stimulus strength.** Unlike clause count, an image corruption can have a specified signal/noise power ratio. It strengthens the analogy at that level. Object identification by one deterministic multimodal checkpoint remains a different task from detection/report by people, and external randomized noise remains different from neural variability.

Correct the anchors: Sergent used **auditory** vowels in threshold-equalizing noise, with five finite SNRs, −13/−11/−9/−7/−5 dB, plus an absent-vowel/noise-only condition. Melcón used brief horizontal/vertical **Gabor patches**, with contrast calibration/adaptation by participant, task and hemifield, jitter and catch trials. Neither supplies a ready photograph dose calibration or seven-level image grid. [Sergent primary paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21393-z), [Melcón primary institutional PDF](https://eprints.gla.ac.uk/316871/1/316871.pdf), [ds006171 description](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/OpenNeuroDatasets/ds006171/master/dataset_description.json)

My first choice is **additive noise with a specified delivered SNR**, addressing the text/SNR objection directly. One possible construction is `I(a)=mu+a*S+sqrt(1−a²)*N`, with a global fixed background `mu`, standardized centered photograph signal `S`, and independent zero-mean noise `N` of equal prescribed RMS. For intermediate `a`, component-power SNR is `10 log10(a²/(1−a²))`; zero/full endpoints are separate limiting conditions, not finite dB values. Specify colour space, noise spectrum, RMS, resizing, clipping and quantization. Measure the delivered values; random cross terms mean a constant expected power is not exactly constant power in every realization. Do not retain each photograph's own mean colour at zero, or derive its zero mask from that photograph's spectrum: those choices retain target information.

| Alternative | What it measures and costs |
|---|---|
| Contrast | Closest to Melcón's manipulated variable. At zero gives a constant canvas; repeated zero inputs are duplicates. Contrast in a model tensor is not a human display threshold. Suitable separately registered alternative. |
| Phase scrambling | Can preserve spatial-frequency amplitude while disrupting structure, but a photograph-specific spectrum may retain identity at “zero”; preserving colour/shape information complicates interpretation. |
| Occlusion | Manipulates visible location/area, so diagnostic object parts and clutter vary with the mask. It is not a simple energy/SNR continuum and should not be the first transfer test. |

Keep **seven ordered levels**, zero/full plus five finite strengths, as design logic. The values need not be `{0,1,2,3,4,6,8}` or equally spaced amplitudes/dB. Place them using development/CAL and an independent PILOT, then freeze a global grid for CONF. Model-specific per-CONF-concept staircases would select dose from the very system being tested, remove some threshold heterogeneity and change the target. I would not use them for the first arm. If independently normed per-image calibration is later adopted, declare that conditional population and simulate the calibration procedure.

Noise is independent **between draws**. A single realization may be reused across levels within a concept/photo/carrier block to permit matched dose contrasts, with the induced dependence retained; alternatively use independent level masks and declare that design. Primary draws across concepts remain independent. C1 can reuse its target trial's mask for a matched foil control without sharing that mask with the foil concept's separate primary trial.

## 2. Lens, positions, band and causal readout

**E1 and R2 algebra survive; their empirical interpretation needs qualification.** The same tokenizer, language weights/unembedding and frozen `J_l` permit `(W_U[t]−W_U[f])·J_l h_l` after image conditioning. That is a meaningful candidate token-content probe, not a calibrated probability and not proof that a text-averaged Jacobian transports image states well. The actual preferred lens exists with `n_prompts=1000`, `d_model=5120`, J0–J62; retain it for the transfer test, with layer 63 descriptive/plain-logit only. Do not fit an image lens on CONF or silently substitute another lens.

The future image span must precede the marker. A processor-aware builder must record **expanded IDs, image-grid dimensions and multimodal rotary positions**, then derive all marker/edit/suffix indices from that complete sequence. Inserting an image placeholder into text IDs does not supply visual features. Preserve the three-token `[END]` marker, R1/R2's last marker token, H3's three edit positions, and R3's last suffix-token logits. If a native processor/chat template is required, freeze its exact tokens, thinking/report settings and matched text-channel prompt convention as an amendment. Validate the marker's causal independence from the later suffix. Recheck E1 in that actual answer convention, and freeze the answer set's real size/chance rather than carrying the old 127/128 labels.

The pilot must establish held-out full-strength identification, target–foil separation on **matched image controls**, and dose ordering. It must also include **same-family alternatives**: a family recognizer can pass a cross-family target–foil bridge. An R1 decoder can trivially distinguish noise from natural-image statistics; endpoint accuracy and monotonicity alone cannot make it an access readout. Retain target-specific diagnostics and the later causal test.

Keep 23–57 as the proposed primary band, with early 3–15 and late 58–62 diagnostics. Do not require a positive mixture score to pass development. If image information is strongest elsewhere, or the text lens fails despite a valid image-trained decoder, distinguish probe failure from absence of representation. A new CAL/PILOT-selected image band is a prospective different test; a CONF-selected band cannot rescue the claim of a common predeclared band.

**H3 is transferable in form, not unchanged as an experiment.** Token-direction edits can operate on the downstream language stream, but image CAL amplitudes, layer choices, norm matching and clean-image positive-control patches require fresh qualification. The positive patch must use the same photograph, carrier and geometry at full strength. State assignment and the state-versus-smooth comparison remain held-out and gated. Failure of this text-derived direction is not proof that the visual content lacks causal use; success concerns use by the downstream answer path, not localization of the threshold to that path.

C1 becomes a matched foil photograph at the same strength/canvas/carrier/noise. Its scene background is not automatically identical: use independently audited isolated objects on a common background or declare and balance that difference. C2 cannot be inherited from counting competitor clauses; blended/side-by-side competing objects change area and token geometry and need their own specification. The first pilot below omits C2 and scientific H3. Full confirmation requires their intended image-specific declarations. Also retain the prior H3 availability correction: an off-target pair from **two other families** requires four total background families, or external background families, when target and foil occupy two.

## 3. Variability, photographs and the sampling unit

**NOT ON BOARD with “noise draws give exchangeability” without the hierarchy.** The unit is a concept sampled within a defined family/source population. Its cluster contains photographs, corruption draws, levels, carriers and layers. Noise draws provide repeated conditional inputs; they neither create a population of checkpoints nor turn a selected object bank into a random sample of English concepts.

Use separate symbols `P` for distinct photographs and `R` for noise realizations. Text's D is a packet-draw count, not automatically either of these. **No exact photograph count is implied by the existing interval.** For a claim about recognition beyond a single exemplar, use multiple independently sourced views: two is the minimum that can expose an exemplar difference; **four is a sensible planning target**, with held-out-exemplar checks, not a proven sufficient P. Crops, flips and masks of one photograph do not supply four exemplars. One CC 0 photograph supports a narrower inference conditional on the chosen image for each sampled concept.

Photo-specific thresholds, object size/viewpoint, recognizability, clutter, background removal, OCR/labels, brightness/colour and the vision encoder's own nonlinearity are additional causes of variation. Photographs sharing a shoot/source/background can also be dependent. Randomize or balance them and report the source hierarchy. Preprocessing must fix canvas, visual-token grid and all text positions across dose, including zero; removing the image at zero creates a different sequence.

The likelihood in §8 multiplies trial densities conditional on a concept effect. Repeated observations of one photograph can retain a shared photo effect beyond that assumption. Either add the photo structure to **both** competing families or stress-test the proposed unchanged families against graded photo heterogeneity and within-photo dependence. Clustering the bootstrap at concept level does not repair a misspecified density model or omitted fitting uncertainty.

At full strength the noise term disappears: repeated mask seeds yield identical inputs for a fixed photo/carrier. Contrast-zero similarly yields a constant image. Deduplicate these in the inferential dataset or declare justified observation weights; they consume requests, not new evidence. Carry complete clusters through folds and refitting. Paired C1/bridge statistics retain their prescribed pair-level resampling; do not silently create common-mask dependence between separate primary concept clusters.

## 5. Audit transfer, calibration and the two-arm hypothesis

**The original audit findings stand without rerunning them just because a new arm is proposed.** The scalar generators and statistical code remain useful abstract tests; original null calls, sensitivity and reference-inclusion failure remain results of their stated designs. They do not certify an arbitrary scalar distribution. In particular, the failed fixed-score interval must not be revived for images. A valid replacement, fresh-seed/reference-uncertainty gates and the existing dispatch controls remain prerequisites to confirmation.

Before image confirmation, qualify:

- The actual physical dose, extreme-trained image R1, frozen R2 content probe and image controls; the scalar audit did not simulate image→encoder→residual→learned projection.
- Gain calibration and power at the actual concept count, P/R/carrier/level design and readout scale/heterogeneity, including photo effects and relevant misspecification. Merely increasing n_c is not an equivalent design.
- The interval with pipeline refitting as required, explicit estimand and reference precision. State whether the target conditions on frozen CAL instruments or includes their training variability; bootstrap/simulation must agree with that target.
- Band averaging under **measured image cross-layer dependence**. One-layer results are not 35-layer validation. Decoder/layer/scaling selection stays outside CONF, with numerical fitting checks and common missing/undefined-control policies.

CONF 64/CAL 16/PILOT 16 **per arm is a defensible retained target**, not an inherited image power proof. More images do not replace 64 concepts. Prefer the same concepts and role assignments across arms when making a paired modality comparison: a concept cannot be text CONF and image CAL. Carry both arms through the same concept folds and joint resamples; shared 64 concepts remain 64 units, not 128. If separate banks are needed, report separate source populations and the subset overlap; a modality difference then mixes modality with concept composition. Do not let the text bank's intersection dictate an undisclosed image population or claim all image findings depend on CSLB.

Predeclare the modest cross-modal claim: **both registered arms favour the specified mixture family in the same fixed 23–57 band, with their content/causal preconditions satisfied**. Two positive band means do not establish equal parameters, identical peak location, mechanism or modality independence. Equality needs a specified paired estimand/equivalence margin and calibrated joint analysis; common nats alone do not provide equivalence.

For one all-pass conjunction, calibrated component tests at level α permit an intersection–union error bound ≤α even with dependent arms; automatic Bonferroni across these two necessary gates is not required. This does not fix uncalibrated components. Separate successful-arm claims, either-arm success, choosing a corruption after outcomes or searching bands require a declared multiplicity policy. If one arm is graded with a valid instrument, the conjunction fails; if one arm's readout/control is unqualified, report the conjunction as untested rather than a physiological falsification.

## 7. Cost, identity and what may be claimed

The processor uses RGB, bicubic resize, rescaling by 255 and channel mean/std 0.5. Patch 16 and spatial merge 2 give approximately **H×W/1,024 merged image tokens** at the processed dimensions (multiples of 32). Default pixel bounds allow roughly 64–16,384 image tokens; freeze a smaller geometry explicitly. The vision encoder adds 27 layers, and its outputs traverse the 64 language layers. “4-bit checkpoint” does not establish a 4-bit vision encoder: the local index has no visual quantization-scale/bias entries.

| Square input | Vision patches | Merged image tokens | Retained all-layer residuals with 64 illustrative text IDs |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 256 | 256 | 64 | 80 MiB |
| 384 | 576 | 144 | 130 MiB |
| 512 | 1,024 | 256 | 200 MiB |
| 768 | 2,304 | 576 | 400 MiB |

These are static geometry/byte calculations, `64×sequence_length×5120×2`, **excluding** weights, lens, encoder, cache and scratch. A1024-square image alone gives 1024 visual tokens and exceeds the current 1024-ID cap once text is added. Saving one 64-layer marker residual costs 0.625 MiB; the 1,856-request pilot would be about 1.13 GiB for such residuals alone, before logs/control positions. More vision patches add encoder attention cost as well as language-prefill work. The existing approximately 0.9 s **text** capture rate is not an image estimate; no seconds/image or total capture budget is established by this review.

For confirmation, calculate requests from the actual P/R/dose/carrier design, deduplicated endpoints and controls. Two arms, no-target-report and multi-layer H3 multiply that work. Benchmark representative high-resolution/edited cases before dispatch and bind their memory/runtime to the ledger; no cloud/HPC purchase or launch follows from this record.

The weight artifact can retain the same snapshot identity while the **execution identity changes**. Preserve language/vision weight hashes, quantization and lens; add processor, vision-runtime/package/source hashes, input builder, template/position code and precision/kernel versions. Image doses can have identical text/image-placeholder IDs, so `input_ids_sha256` alone does not identify them. Bind original/transformed/processed-pixel hashes, grid, multimodal positions, expanded IDs and all corruption seeds. A newly loaded image path requires a new qualifying report and startup manifest; do not pool earlier text captures under a falsely identical runtime identity.

A later registered report could establish bounded content-related distributional agreement, or disagreement, across **these two arms on this checkpoint**, conditional on qualified instruments, populations and validated intervals. It could establish causal use by its declared interventions. It cannot infer identical human/model mechanisms, a universal workspace law, temporal ignition from layer depth, phenomenality or self-awareness. A vision-encoder threshold or shared answer bottleneck remains an alternative explanation for matching readout structure.

The **31 October SI paper stays the narrowed audit**. It must not describe a photograph arm as built, adopted, calibrated, captured or confirmed from this design review; promise unchanged image power; report a Melcón result; or use matching future outcomes to expand its present evidence. At most a clearly labelled prospective extension belongs in the later report/design material. Its original audit results and limits stand independently of this proposal.
