### Dose is part of the model, not just its label

For a logistic link, replacing the coordinate by a **positive affine** transform `k′ = a + b k` can preserve the density family if `x0′ = a + b x0`, `κ′ = κ/b`, the threshold-effect SD becomes `b τ`, and both endpoint definitions transform. The implemented estimation map also needs correspondingly transformed starts, jitter, quadrature/numerical controls and any constraints. This is algebraic equivalence of a specified model, not a guarantee that two numerical runs find the same fit. A nonlinear change from level index to amplitude or dB generally changes the candidate curves. For a seven-point irregular grid, logistic in index and logistic in physical strength are different hypotheses.

The source's statement that physical dose “could not” be stated for the human study is wrong. Sergent reports five finite SNRs (−13, −11, −9, −7, −5 dB) plus a noise-only condition. Encoding released levels as an index is an analysis choice, not absence of physical measurements. The prior image review verified this from the [primary paper's methods](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-021-21393-z); Melcón's contrast task is another physical manipulation, not a photograph calibration.

For the proposed `I(a)=μ+aS+sqrt(1−a²)N`, component-power SNR is `10 log10(a²/(1−a²))` at finite interior strengths; no-signal and clean endpoints are −∞ and +∞. Neither is a finite dB level. A feasible prospective coordinate is **`k=8a`**, with catch 0/top 8 and the actual finite strengths declared; this makes the link logistic in amplitude, not dB. It is a proposal, not an implemented amendment. Another finite coordinate or explicit endpoint branch is possible, but must be frozen and checked. Declare nominal component SNR separately from delivered post-resize/clipping/quantization signal/noise statistics.

Static [models.py](../models.py) demonstrates why “physical values drop in” is false: `K_MAX=8`, `K_CATCH=0`; graded means anchor at 8; catch branches test exact equality to zero. Raw 0 dB would be mistaken for catch, and infinite endpoints break finite moment initialization. Starts clip the threshold midpoint by an absolute 0.5, use unit ordered-mixture slopes and fixed-scale jitter. These are coordinate-sensitive. The inspected optimizer call uses L-BFGS-B **without explicit bounds**; do not invent a bounds check as evidence. No fitter was run for this review.

Nor do all X members enforce zero catch occupancy: `M3L` deliberately estimates `π0` with a distinct high catch emission. The inherited `M3` is unordered; the newer ordered members enforce the responsive component above the low component. Preserve those distinctions. An inferred catch component is not evidence of stimulus content when no target information was delivered.

### Photo structure and fitting require more than gain calibration

The current joint concept density is `q_m,c = ∫ ∏_i p_m(y_ci | k_ci,u) p_m(u) du`. Conditional on a single concept effect, its trial terms factor. Several masks/carriers of the same photograph can share a threshold or scale effect that this factorization omits. A simple counterexample to automatic transfer is two easy/hard views per concept: responses can be graded within each view and appear mixture-like when pooled. Concept-cluster resampling preserves observed blocks but does not repair the likelihood's missing photo effect or establish that the X/G distinction identifies a workspace state.

If a photo effect is added, add comparable flexibility to **both** families and declare its integration, joint held-out score and recovery. If the eight are retained unchanged, stress-test them against graded photo/background/source heterogeneity and within-photo dependence, with a transparent limit on the resulting claim. The old twelve in-family graded settings do not cover those alternatives automatically. Gains, error rates, power and band covariance must use the final P/R/carrier/level counts and selected readouts; more trials are not interchangeable with more concepts.

Exact duplicate inputs at clean endpoints, and at contrast-zero blank endpoints, are not new stochastic evidence. Deduplicate or use justified frozen weights; carry endpoint definitions into the target and generators. A constant contrast-zero R1 under a fixed carrier can be a point mass rather than the assumed continuous catch density. This needs explicit treatment or a different input design, not Gaussian uncertainty manufactured by replaying the same input. Noise-only masks can supply genuine external variation without target-dependent pixels, provided seeds/backgrounds and cross-concept independence are declared.

Gain calibration concerns a generator's advantage over a fitted large-sample graded reference; it is not the selected finite-design X-versus-G estimand, optimizer recovery or interval coverage. Qualification must keep these measures separate. The existing failed fixed-score interval is not restored by using a scalar image readout. Later author amendments to row 19 have not been re-reviewed here; the earlier owner go and the earlier review's limits remain as recorded. Prospective validation would report failures and Monte Carlo uncertainty as well as successes, consistent with [Morris, White and Crowther's simulation guidance](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6492164/). This is a specification requirement, not permission to launch those simulations.

## 3. Cross-channel target, registered rule and paired interval

**Register independently fitted per-channel tests first, with a bounded all-pass cross-channel claim.** A joint density with a channel covariate is a prospective secondary model after its restrictions/identification are specified; it is not inherently a stronger test. Pooling can improve precision by imposing shared parameters and can conceal one-channel failure. It must not replace an unfavourable independent-arm result.

For channel `j ∈ {text,photo}`, readout `r ∈ {R1,R2}` and fixed workspace grid `L`, define

`D_j,r,c = |L|⁻¹ Σ_l [(log q_X,j,r,c,l − log q_G,j,r,c,l)/n_j,c]`,

then `T_j,r = Σ_s w_s mean_{c in stratum s} D_j,r,c`, with `w_s` fixed for the intended source/family population and `n_j,c` counting unique inferential rows after the frozen endpoint deduplication/weight policy. Weights `w_s=C_s/C` reproduce the existing equal-concept mean; do not silently substitute trial weighting when channel counts differ. Freeze actual strata/counts rather than inherit the protocol's historical eight equal strata. The score is a joint predictive log score per trial, not a sum of independent trial entropies.

The targets are `θ_j,r = E[T_j,r]` under the declared concept/stimulus sampling and fold/start generation at the final design sizes, **conditional on frozen CAL decoders/scaling/lens, dose grid and execution**. This conditional target is my recommendation for the first registered comparison. It averages the specified CONF fitting/selection process, not retraining the instrument on new CAL data. If generalization over instrument training is desired instead, regenerate both CALs and their selection/scaling in the target reference and resampling/calibration; do not bootstrap CONF alone and claim it.

The primary cross-channel target is the **vector** of the four `θ_j,r` and the two arms' content-bridge contrasts, with an intersection of directional claims. Name the expected target-minus-matched-C1 contrast `ν_j` and the expected fixed-dose high-minus-low contrast `η_j`; their weighting and state-availability policies are part of their definitions. Thus the target is `(θ_text,R1, θ_photo,R1, θ_text,R2, θ_photo,R2, ν_text, ν_photo, η_text, η_photo)`. A scalar difference alone cannot represent the intended both-mixture prediction: zero difference can occur when both channels are graded.

Register the following ordered claim ladder before CONF:

1. **Statistical replication:** qualified R1 comparisons in both arms have calibrated positive intervals excluding zero for the same predeclared 23–57 band/grid.
2. **Content-related replication, recommended primary cross-channel claim:** step 1 plus the full R2 bridge in **each** arm: positive target-versus-matched-C1 separation (§6.4(i)), positive R2 family band result (§8.5(a)), and positive high-minus-low R2 separation at fixed interior dose using held-out R1 state assignments (§8.5(b)). Declare image interior doses; the text labels 2/3/4 cannot silently stand for raw dB values.
3. **Stronger causal-use tier:** step 2 plus each arm's registered H3 interaction, effect-size/control/positive-control and state-versus-smooth qualifications. This is downstream use under the specified interventions, not shared origin of a threshold.

For one conjunction, valid level-α necessary component tests permit an intersection–union error bound ≤α despite paired dependence: if any required null is true, all-pass implies rejecting that true component. This does **not** validate an individual test or justify nominal 5% error from a nominal 95% interval with only a 0.90 inclusion acceptance floor. Validate the component decisions and the actual compound policy. Declare how weaker-tier standalone claims, either-arm claims, optional degradations/readouts/bands and equivalence/localization analyses are controlled; the all-pass argument does not cover those opportunities automatically.

### Paired refitting construction

Use the same CONF concept roles and concept-disjoint inner/outer fold assignments in both arms. A concept cannot be CAL in one arm and CONF in the other. For each refitting bootstrap replicate:

1. Draw one concept-index/multiplicity vector within the **actual registered family strata**; apply it to both channels. Carry each concept's complete photo/text trials, masks, levels, carriers, layers and channel correspondence. Do not resample the two arms independently.
2. Preserve bootstrap multiplicity as repeated joint-cluster contributions or frequency weights: a concept sampled `b_c` times contributes `b_c log q_model,c`. Keep **all copies of its original identity together in both outer and inner folds**, and pair that grouping across channels. Pooling copies into one enlarged random-effect integral gives `log∫∏p^b_c p(u)du`, which differs from `b_c log∫∏p p(u)du`; do not deduplicate away multiplicity or allow an identical copy in training and held-out scoring.
3. Rerun each arm's density fits, nested selection, outer refits, joint scores, frozen-grid averaging and common failure policy. CAL instruments remain frozen for the conditional target. Use a declared paired fold/seed construction and channel-specific fit streams. Record empty/insufficient strata, unscorable fits and failed replicates; do not discard them until an interval looks favourable.
4. Store the paired four-band-score vector `T_b`. A candidate is the marginal 2.5%/97.5% percentile interval for each score, subject to the validation below. For a secondary paired magnitude contrast `δ_r = θ_photo,r − θ_text,r`, compute `δ_b = T_photo,r,b − T_text,r,b` **within each replicate**, then form its interval. Subtracting endpoints of independently constructed intervals is not this paired construction.

The protocol's B=200 replacement is a proposal to qualify, not an assurance of stable bootstrap tails or coverage for this new hierarchy: only about five draws occupy each 2.5% tail. The final method, replicate count, quantile convention, failure policy and finite-design coverage/reference precision must pass prospective validation including cross-arm, photo and layer dependence. Regenerating/refitting in a bootstrap is necessary to address the previously omitted learning variability; it is not by itself a coverage theorem. A whole-concept resample supports the declared concept-cluster population. A claim about new photographs from a photo population needs photo sampling represented in that design/reference; adding an arbitrary second resampling stage can double-count or change the target.

**Foil/bridge intervals need their own declared dependence treatment.** The existing protocol explicitly uses an unstratified reciprocal-foil-pair bootstrap for paired diagnostics because foil pairs cross family strata. Preserve the text/image versions of each pair together; recompute fitted state assignments wherever their learning variability belongs to the bridge target. If multiple concepts share control materials or other induced dependence, use the declared larger block/connected group, or redesign the primary draw independence; independent-concept resampling is not automatically valid for that statistic. Do not force a cross-family foil pair into one family stratum or substitute its interval for the R1/R2 concept-score interval. These bridge marginal intervals can support the conjunction without pretending that their distinct resampling schemes form one joint bootstrap law with the four band scores.

Report `δ_r` as a **secondary procedural contrast** under these instruments/designs, not an effect of modality in isolation. Common nats, paired concepts and z-scaling do not make two learned readouts the same measurement. If equivalence is wanted, choose a substantively justified margin `ε_r` before CONF, validate the paired contrast, and require its prespecified equivalence interval wholly within `[-ε_r,+ε_r]`; neither a zero-containing interval nor two matching signs shows equivalence. No numerical margin is justified by this brief. Likewise, a joint covariate analysis requires explicitly declared channel-dependent slopes/emissions/random effects, measurement links and restriction being tested, with concept-disjoint nested validation. Leave an unspecified joint model out of the confirmatory claim.

## 4. Encoder confound: diagnostics in the pilot, no observational settlement

The forward order is pixels→vision blocks→patch merger/projector→inserted visual embeddings→language layers→answer. Official Qwen reference code exposes pre-merger `last_hidden_state` and post-merger `pooler_output`; the latter is a **sequence** of projected visual tokens, not one scalar pool. The matching installed Transformers version is 5.13.0; widths are 1,152 before merger and 5,120 after. The earlier static snapshot review found 27 vision blocks and 64 language layers. These architecture facts do not establish a two-state vision threshold. [Pinned reference implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/transformers/blob/v5.13.0/src/transformers/models/qwen3_5/modeling_qwen3_5.py), [official configuration](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen3.6-27B/blob/main/config.json)

Correct the causal sentence. An **upstream** encoder cannot smooth a later workspace state into a graded observation on the same forward pass. It can reshape the evidence delivered downstream; later computation, overlapping emissions and scalar projection can hide or transform structure. If an encoder-only readout is mixture-supported but the workspace readout is graded, that concerns two measurements at two stages. It neither demonstrates an inaccessible workspace state nor proves where a threshold was created.

No encoder-only dose curve settles causal origin, **before or during the pilot**. Comparing independently qualified scalar readouts at encoder output and band can diagnose stage-associated differences/propagation, but cannot uniquely identify origin. Mixture support in both could be inherited or separately induced; encoder graded/band mixture is consistent with downstream change but also with different probe sensitivity. A null encoder probe cannot show that the full high-dimensional encoder representation lacks relevant structure. A mean dose curve, sigmoid or argmax jump is not evidence of two latent states.

**Include bounded encoder diagnostics in the proposed native-first pilot.** They supplement, not replace, its fidelity/content gates:

- In Stage A, verify the processed pixels, visual-token grid/ordering, finite projected features and insertion/positions against the native path. Record the post-merger boundary and a pre-merger diagnostic if practical. Compare repeated/empty-edit paths under the declared precision. The existing text-only adapter has no image implementation yet; these are requirements for its future qualification, not an observed pass.
- In Stage B, record the projected visual-token sequence or a prospectively frozen summary on the **same existing trials** as band/answer readouts. Define the stage, tensor width, normalization and pooling before inspecting PILOT. A CAL-only endpoint discriminator with grouped CV/frozen scaling is an encoder R1 diagnostic; it detects input strength/quality and is not automatically semantic. The language-layer lens is not inherited for vision tokens.
- Report per-concept/photo/mask dose profiles, held-out endpoint discriminability and their paired relation to band R1/R2 and R3. A prespecified cross-view target-versus-foil representation-similarity diagnostic can be informative if its reference/pooling is fixed and validated; same-photo similarity chiefly tracks image retention, and no encoder content measure should be invented from its name. Freeze any mixture/graded encoder comparison as developmental/secondary with its own densities and uncertainty.
- Keep the existing instrument go criteria: native fidelity, held-out recognition/target control, meaningful interior doses and finite bounded execution. Do **not** require encoder or workspace mixture support to proceed. A failed encoder diagnostic prompts probe/input-path diagnosis; it does not independently falsify visual representation. Repeated tuning requires fresh held-out qualification.

Boundary saving/pooling adds implementation/storage cost even if no extra forward is needed. Admit it under the previously proposed Stage-A/Stage-B aggregate ceilings (at most 2,056 requests/eight hours) only after measured capacity; otherwise amend the prospective design before running. This record adds no dispatch permission, capture request or causal experiment.

A stronger origin claim requires separately designed interventions: for example, controlled substitutions at the visual-feature boundary, matched norm/amplitude/sham controls, corresponding downstream interventions and preserved recognition. Such manipulations can test causal influence, with off-distribution and projection limits; even a positive boundary substitution is not automatically unique localization. H3 as currently proposed concerns downstream answer use and does not settle encoder origin. This additional causal design is later work, not a prerequisite to an honestly bounded recognition pilot.

## 5. Referee omissions and required author update

The follow-on should explicitly address:

- **Measurement validity before model selection.** Endpoint R1 can distinguish noise from natural-image statistics; even a cross-family R2 contrast can reflect family recognition. Retain same-family identification, matched C1 content controls, zero-information construction and downstream causal/smooth-account limits. Shared answer-token bottlenecks can produce matching readouts without a shared mechanism.
- **Dose/source population.** Global versus per-image calibration changes the population and threshold heterogeneity; CONF-specific tuning is not a neutral relabeling. Eligible bank intersections and independently cleared views define the paired target. Shared concepts do not make external mask variability equivalent to human neural variability. THINGS transformations, multiple views, native runtime and accepted usable counts remain gates owned by their respective lanes.
- **Design/likelihood coherence.** Endpoint duplicates/atoms, photo/source random effects, masks reused across doses, controls and unequal counts must match both the likelihood and uncertainty target. All retained members and numerical failures remain visible. Adding flexibility to only one family can manufacture the preferred description.
- **Band and execution identity.** Same weights do not establish native-path parity, a usable common band or validity of a text-trained lens. Qualify both channels under the declared execution/template/marker convention. A changed text runtime or prompt is another design factor. Fixed layer 41 pilot diagnostics do not validate the full 23–57 band; no CONF peak selection.
- **Prospective error/claim policy.** Validate the final full procedure and relevant graded misspecification, not just gain. Specify vector targets, frozen CAL conditionality, paired folds/refits, controls, unavailability, claim tiers, multiplicity and independent reference uncertainty. Existing audit/row-19 approvals are not an image-run purchase authorization. Prior pilot/audit exposure remains disclosed.

For framework §10, replace “transfers” in the predictor/fitter/unit/uncertainty/rule verdict cells with **“proposed instance; re-derivation and qualification required”**, retaining the concrete algebra that survives. Remove the monotone-decoder claim, human-physical-dose absence claim, universal `A(0)=0`, automatic exchangeability and reversed encoder causality. Replace minimal “same description” agreement with the registered bounded hierarchy above. Keep the two added declaration headings, and leave the narrowed SI audit alone.

**What would make me on board with the operational assay:** a prospective owner specification that fixes physical input and coordinate/endpoints, independently qualifies native readouts and content controls, represents or challenges photo dependence symmetrically, qualifies fitting/refitting/error behavior at the intended design, and freezes the paired targets/rules and their outcome readings. Those requirements organize a revised proposal. No scientific outcome, calibration pass, implementation or campaign approval is supplied by this review.
