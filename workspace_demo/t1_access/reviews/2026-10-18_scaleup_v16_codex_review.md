CHPC documents `mem` as RAM per node; PBS's virtual-memory accounting and enforcement also depend on the site's hooks. The selected fields do not establish Lengau's hook configuration, a separate virtual-memory limit or swap activity. Therefore “PBS enforces only mem here” is not independently proved by this package. The completed job and low observed `mem` support this decision without that stronger claim.
