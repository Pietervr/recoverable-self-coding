### B2 — P2: opposite adaptive-count corners are not exact coverage bounds

`row19_screen_release_p80.py:139–142`, its docstring `:43–44`, and `RELEASE_P80.md` §4 item 4 advertise exact lower and upper CP bounds at **both** Kmax and Kmin. These counts select their target from the observed intervals and are not generally binomial counts at a fixed target.

For fixed theta_g within T, the valid confidence sandwich is **L_CP(Kmin,n) to U_CP(Kmax,n)**. Its opposite corners, L_CP(Kmax,n) and U_CP(Kmin,n), do not inherit exact coverage guarantees. The primary futility calculation uses the valid upper corner and is unaffected by this reporting defect.

Exact counterexample: 60 independent intervals are `[0,0]` or `[1,1]`, each with probability 1/2; T is `[0,1]`. At the fixed true target 0, coverage is .5. L_CP(Kmax,60) exceeds .5 with probability **.02734013**, above the claimed .015 error. Kmin is always zero and its reported upper CP bound is **.06760160**, although that target's coverage is .5. These are deterministic binomial calculations, not simulation.

**Fix:** report the valid sandwich with its one-sided 98.5% guarantees, and a two-sided 97% interpretation under the same binomial assumptions conditional on theta_g lying in T. The bank's nominal 1% error is separate: that two-sided descriptive envelope has a nominal combined error bound up to 4%, while primary directional futility retains 1.5% +1% =2.5%. Remove the opposite corners or explicitly label them noninferential transforms. Retain the attempted-set envelope's valid lower-at-Kmin and upper-at-Kmax-plus-M corners. Add a deterministic selected-count counterexample test.

### B4 — P2: floating midpoints can miss the true minimum overlap

`row19_screen_release_p80.py:110–123` samples numerical midpoints to compute Kmin. Between adjacent floating values the midpoint can round to an endpoint, omitting a genuine open segment of the real target set.

For intervals `[(0.0,1.0), (nextafter(1.0,+inf),2.0)]` and T=`[0,2]`, the code returns Kmin=1. Yet the exact real target **9007199254740993/9007199254740992 =1+2^-53** lies in the gap and is in neither interval: the true minimum is zero. This overstates the lower empirical/confidence envelope. Kmax and the primary futility rule are unaffected.

**Fix:** compute counts at closed endpoints and on symbolic open segments with a sorted endpoint sweep; do not require a representable midpoint. Keep inclusive ties correct. Add adjacent-float, touching-endpoint and large-finite-value/overflow fixtures. Relabeling the CP corners under B2 alone does not repair this calculation.
